Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Welcome to Meta Leads PoC Demo. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, stored and used when you submit our demonstration Facebook Instant Form.

1. About This Project

Meta Leads PoC Demo is a technical demonstration project designed to test the integration of Meta Lead Ads with a backend service, Supabase database and React Native application. It is not a commercial lead-generation service.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit our demonstration form, we may receive:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Meta lead, Page and form identifiers
  • Form submission date and time

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Testing Meta Lead Ads webhook integration.
  • Demonstrating real-time lead delivery.
  • Displaying submitted leads in our React Native application.
  • Testing backend processing and database functionality.

We do not use this demonstration to send unsolicited marketing communications or sell personal information.

4. Data Storage and Processing

Submitted information is processed by our backend server and stored in a Supabase database for demonstration and testing purposes. Access is restricted to authorized project operators.

Meta and Supabase may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is processed through Meta's services and Supabase as necessary to operate this demonstration.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain submitted test information only for as long as necessary for the demonstration. Records will be deleted when they are no longer required.

You may contact us to request access to or deletion of information submitted through this demonstration.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored information and restrict unauthorized access. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may choose not to submit the demonstration form. You may also contact us with questions or requests concerning your submitted information.
